- Spearman(residual, cnv_score)=0.475, p~0: residual and CNV score are correlated, as
  expected since both derive from related signal, but far from 1 -- most residual variation
  is not explained by overall CNV burden.

  clone 2 (no notable arm event) has the lowest ASCL2+ rate (15.8%) and lowest median

  rates (80-85%) despite different top-decile enrichment (obs/exp 1.26, 1.46, 0.41).

  threshold, and eta^2 (0.071) is below the 0.3 state threshold used for a clean call in

  low), which favors the state side of that rule. Verdict printed above: STATE hypothesis
  better supported.
