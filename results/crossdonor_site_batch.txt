CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- Among the 7 qualifying donors, only 5 share the same tissue (sigmoid colon); the
  other 2 (caecum, hepatic flexure) are each unique in this cohort and have no
  same-tissue partner, so their site percentile is N/A -- the same-tissue distribution
  is really a same-tissue-among-5-donors comparison, not a fully general one.
- Harmony integration is run over the FULL cohort (all donors, normal+malignant) so its
  correction is influenced by all cells, not just the 7 analyzed here.
- Harmony can remove real biological (including disease-relevant) variation along with
  batch effects; a shrinking malignant ratio after Harmony does not by itself mean the
  disease signal was 'batch', only that Harmony's correction affected it too.
- All numbers here are derived from the same expression matrix as the residual under
  test -- internal consistency, not independent validation.
- This does not revisit scripts/heldout_crossdonor_control.py's own results/verdict;
  it is a follow-up mechanism check only.
