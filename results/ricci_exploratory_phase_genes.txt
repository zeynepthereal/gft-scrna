CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- ORC (kappa), residual, and gene expression are all derived from the same 50-D PCA / log-normalized expression matrix. Correlations with genes that dominate the PCA are partly mechanical for this reason; the local_ID comparison is the main control against that, not an independent validation.
- n_genes_by_counts is also associated with stemness/CytoTRACE-like scores; using it as a covariate here may partially remove genuine biological signal along with the sequencing-depth confound, in either direction.
- HTA8_6002 has only 60 malignant cells -- statistical power is low for this donor specifically, and its per-gene partial correlations are the least stable of the six.
- This entire analysis is exploratory and follows a failed pre-registered robustness check (T1, 3/6 donors); nothing here should be read as confirmatory.
