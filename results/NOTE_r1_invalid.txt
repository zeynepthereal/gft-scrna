NOTE — the "R1" normal-only-space robustness check (results/geom_ppca_potential.txt,
results/geom_principal_angles.txt) is INVALID BY DESIGN and its conclusions should not be relied
upon. Nothing in those two files has been edited; this note documents the flaw and supersedes
their R1-based conclusions going forward.

THE FLAW
--------
R1's "normal-only space" fit a 50-D PCA on normal cells' top-2000 HVGs, then projected malignant
cells into that fixed subspace. Projecting a point onto a subspace by construction discards all
variance orthogonal to that subspace. But the projection residual under test — the entire quantity
this thesis is about — IS the out-of-subspace variance. A normal-cell-only subspace, by
definition, cannot contain directions that are specific to malignant cells; projecting malignant
cells into it necessarily throws away exactly the signal (large, malignant-specific out-of-
subspace variance) that the residual is supposed to detect. The "normal-only space" was not a
leakage-free control — it was a test with its own answer built into its construction, biased
toward finding LESS separation regardless of the true underlying biology.

This means:
  - geom_ppca_potential.py's "normal_only" B1 results (2/6 donors passing, several wildly
    unstable/negative share_residual values such as -4.25) do not show that the residual finding
    is unreliable — the test itself could not have shown otherwise had the residual finding been
    completely real, because the 50-D truncation to a normal-only-derived subspace pre-discards
    the very signal being measured.
  - geom_principal_angles.py's "normal_only" B2a results are affected the same way.
  - The "cohort PCA leaks / R1 shows the malignant signal is inflated" narrative given at the end
    of the geom_ppca_potential.py / geom_principal_angles.py summary is WITHDRAWN. It does not
    follow from the R1 comparison as constructed.

WHAT IS AND ISN'T INVALIDATED
------------------------------
- The cohort-PCA-space results in geom_ppca_potential.py and geom_principal_angles.py are NOT
  invalidated by this note. Cohort PCA is unsupervised (fit without using the normal/malignant
  label at all) and is not the flawed part of R1; only the specific "normal-only 50-D projection"
  construction used as a control is flawed.
- B2b's cohort-PCA-space result (observed cross-donor consistency 0.1284 vs null p95 0.1287,
  NOT SUPPORTED) is unaffected by this note and stands as reported. It is not retested here (see
  scripts/geom_r1_fullgene.py's docstring: B2b is closed and is not reopened by relaxing the space
  it was tested in).
- A corrected, scalable version of the full-gene-space idea (no truncation to 50 dimensions, no
  early projection that discards malignant-specific variance) is implemented in
  scripts/geom_r1_fullgene.py -> results/geom_r1_fullgene.txt.

A second, independent correction addressed in the same follow-up step: the previous PPCA delta-E
decomposition used MEDIAN differences, which are not additive across the residual and M_in terms
(this produced the impossible -4.25 "share" value in the invalid normal-only space above). The
corrected script uses MEAN differences, which decompose exactly.
