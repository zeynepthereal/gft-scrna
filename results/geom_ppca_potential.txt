================================================================================================
CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- E, r, and M_in are all derived from the same normal-cell PPCA fit and the same PCA
  coordinates as the residual itself; this is an internal decomposition, not an
  independent measurement.
- The malignant-vs-normal separation is an easy problem in general (both terms and
  n_genes alone give high AUROC in most donors); the comparison of AUROCs to each other,
  not their absolute level, is what the decision rule uses.
- HTA8_6002 has only 60 malignant cells and 264 normal cells -- its per-fold held-out
  normal groups (~53 cells/fold) are small, and its estimates are the least stable.
- The normal-only space's HVG selection and PCA are refit independently for this
  script; they are not guaranteed to match the exact cohort PCA subspace used elsewhere
  in the repo even for the same donor, by construction (that is the point of space ii).
