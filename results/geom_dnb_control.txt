CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- Distribution matching equalizes SD_in (and the module-score range) between bins by
  construction, so it is expected to shrink both I and PCC_in toward the reference (Q1)
  bin's values; a genuine reduction in the raw-to-matched gap does not by itself prove
  the raw finding was purely an artefact, only that heterogeneity explains part of it.
- When a bin lacks enough cells in one of Q1's score deciles, cells are resampled WITH
  replacement to keep counts equal across reps; donors/modules where this occurred are
  flagged inline.
- Residual, module scores and gene expression are derived from the same expression
  matrix; this is an internal-consistency test, not independent validation.
- Residual quantiles in this snapshot dataset are not a time axis.
- HTA8_6002 has only 60 malignant cells and uses 3 bins; it has by far the lowest power.
