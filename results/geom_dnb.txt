CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- Residual, module scores, and gene expression are all derived from the same expression
  matrix. This is an internal-consistency test, not independent validation.
- scRNA dropout depresses correlation estimates. Binomial thinning to a common depth
  equalizes this effect across bins, but does not remove the dropout floor itself.
- Residual quantiles in this snapshot dataset are not a time axis. A Q1->Q5 ordering can
  only be read as a hypothesized trajectory, not an observed one.
- HTA8_6002 has only 60 malignant cells: 3 bins (terciles) were used instead of 5, and
  its per-bin subsample size (after matching to the smallest group) is small -- this
  donor has by far the lowest power in Component 3.
- The secondary (exploratory) hierarchical-clustering modules are capped to the top
  2000 most variable donor-expressed genes for computational tractability; this is a
  narrower gene universe than the full >=10%-expressed filter used for Hallmark modules,
  and is not part of the pre-registered B3 decision rule.
