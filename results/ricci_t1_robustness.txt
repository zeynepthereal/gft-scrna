================================================================================================
CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- HTA8_6002 has only 60 malignant cells (smallest donor) and no tumor-non-malignant
  group (0 cells) -- criterion (c) is not-applicable there, and all its per-seed graphs
  are built on only 60 cells per group, so its per-donor estimates are noisier than the
  other 5 donors.
- The tumor-non-malignant group's sample size is capped at min(n, N_tumor_nonmal); for
  HTA8_6018 (n=54 available) and HTA8_6009/6013/6028 this is well below the matched
  normal/malignant n, so its per-seed graphs are smaller and its ORC estimates in 1d
  are correspondingly less stable than the normal/malignant comparison.
- 1b's OLS is fit on the same combined graph the pilot used for its own T1 (no
  subsampling) -- it is not independent of the pilot's original T1 result, only an
  extension of the same regression with two more covariates.
- local_ID (Levina-Bickel) and knn_dist are themselves computed from the same 50-D PCA
  coordinates that the kNN graph and curvature are built from -- they are covariates of
  the same geometry, not an independent measurement.
