================================================================================================
CAVEATS
================================================================================================
- The cross-donor consistency test (b) is only meaningful in the cohort PCA space, which
  is the one shared basis across donors; the normal-only space uses a different,
  donor-specific HVG set and PCA fit for every donor, so 'new direction' vectors from
  different donors in that space are not directly comparable and are not tested for (b).
- 'Average cos^2' between two donors' new-direction subspaces is computed as the mean
  cos^2 of the principal angles BETWEEN THE TWO 3-D SUBSPACES (via SVD(Nd^T Nd')), not as
  a naive column-by-column cosine, since individual N_d column vectors have no canonical
  ordering beyond spanning the subspace.
- Gene weights, the consensus direction, and any enrichment are derived from the same
  expression matrix used to build the PCA subspaces; this is an internal-consistency
  analysis, not independent validation.
- HTA8_6002 (60 malignant, 264 normal cells) gives the noisiest per-donor subspace fits
  of the six, most visibly in the sampling-noise null (smallest half-split n).
