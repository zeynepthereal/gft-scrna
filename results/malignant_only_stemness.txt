SUMMARY OF CHECK 1 AND CHECK 2 (facts only, no interpretation beyond the numbers above)
##########################################################################################

CHECK 1 (formula sanity check):
- scripts/malignant_only_stemness.py imports residual()/subspace_svd() directly from
  scripts/paper_final_verification.py (the centered formula) and never reimplements them.
- Recomputing the ratio on ALL carcinoma epithelial cells (no malignant restriction), via
  this exact code path, gave 3.67x for Patient1 and 1.39x for Patient2 -- an exact match to
  the Mac rebuild verification reference. No bug found; no fix was needed. The 3.94x and
  2.11x figures reported for the malignant-only subsets in the previous run are real and
  expected: they are the ratio computed on the smaller, normal-like-cell-excluded population,
  not the same quantity as the ~3.67x/1.39x whole-population reference, so they are not
  comparable to each other.

CHECK 2 (CRC cohort) -- a real bug was found and fixed while implementing this check: the
  original step2_crc_cohort() masked normal cells with `cell_type == "normal"`, but this
  dataset's cell_type column has no "normal" category (only epithelial subtypes: colonocyte,
  secretory cell, etc.) -- that mask would have silently returned 0 normal cells for every
  donor. Fixed to use `disease == "normal"` (this dataset's actual normal/tumor label),
  matching the approach in scripts/paper_final_verification.py's run_crc(). This is now noted
  explicitly in the script's Step 2 output.
- 7/9 donors passed the >=50 normal / >=50 malignant filter (HTA8_6011 and HTA8_6014 failed,
  both for <50 normal cells).
- Only 3/9 samples overall (Patient1, HTA8_6002, HTA8_6013) show positive AND significant
  Spearman(residual, ASCL2) within malignant cells -- by the stated rule, the ASCL2 stemness
  claim DOES NOT SURVIVE restriction to malignant-only cells across this expanded sample set.
- SOX4 behaves differently: 6/9 samples (Patient1, HTA8_6002, HTA8_6009, HTA8_6016, HTA8_6018,
  HTA8_6028) show positive AND significant Spearman(residual, SOX4) -- a majority, unlike
  ASCL2. The two stemness markers do not agree with each other in most CRC donors; SOX4 tracks
  residual more consistently than ASCL2 does once normal-like/non-malignant cells are removed.
- Quality confound differs by dataset: in Patient1/Patient2 (GSE161277, raw-count pct_mt),
  pct_counts_mt showed no correlation with residual. In the CRC cohort, pct_counts_mt is
  significantly NEGATIVELY correlated with residual in several donors (HTA8_6009 rho=-0.445
  p=8e-39, HTA8_6013 rho=-0.318 p=4e-17, HTA8_6016 rho=-0.126 p=4e-4) -- lower-residual cells
  in these donors have relatively higher mitochondrial content, a pattern often associated
  with cell stress/death, not simply "low quality/low complexity." n_genes_by_counts also
  behaves oppositely from the GSE161277 patients: it is POSITIVELY correlated with residual in
  5/7 CRC donors (rho 0.198-0.577, all p<0.02), including a strong rho=0.577 in HTA8_6002 (the
  smallest malignant group, n=64). This is the opposite direction from Patient1/Patient2's
  small negative correlations, and is large enough in HTA8_6002 to be a genuine quality
  confound candidate: in that donor, higher-residual cells reliably have more detected genes,
  which is at least as plausibly a library-complexity effect as a biological one. This
  confound pattern was not present in the GSE161277 patients and should be weighed when
  comparing residual findings across the two data sources.
- Tissue split (primary colon vs liver metastasis) was possible for 4/7 donors (HTA8_6009,
  HTA8_6016, HTA8_6018, HTA8_6028; the other 3 lacked >=50 cells in one tissue). The
  Moorman et al. expectation -- that primary tumors retain a stronger LGR5+/stem-like
  (ASCL2) association while metastases lose intestinal identity -- is NOT consistently
  supported by these four donors:
    HTA8_6009: primary rho=-0.147 (p=1.2e-4), met rho=-0.202 (p=0.062, not significant) --
      both negative, similar direction; no primary>met asymmetry.
    HTA8_6016: primary rho=+0.064 (n.s.), met rho=-0.048 (n.s.) -- both near zero, neither
      significant; no evidence either way.
    HTA8_6018: primary rho=+0.071 (p=1.9e-3), met rho=+0.183 (p=1.4e-6) -- BOTH positive and
      significant, with the metastasis correlation numerically STRONGER than the primary --
      the opposite of the expected direction.
    HTA8_6028: primary rho=+0.042 (n.s.), met rho=-0.079 (p=0.043) -- weakly in the expected
      direction (primary slightly higher than met), but the primary correlation itself is not
      significant.
  Across these 4 donors, only HTA8_6028 shows a pattern even loosely consistent with the
  Moorman et al. prediction, and its primary-tissue correlation is not itself significant.
  HTA8_6018 shows the opposite pattern. This check does not support the ASCL2-residual
  association being reliably stronger in primary tumors than in liver metastases.
